# Data sources

Status as of 11 November 2026. "Pending" items are tasks for the research team.

## Daily indicators (`data/indicators.json`)

| Indicator | Ranges used | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resting heart rate | 60–100 bpm normal | Pending source |
| Blood pressure | below 120/80 normal; 120–139 / 80–89 watch | Pending source |
| Body temperature | 36.1–37.2 °C normal | Pending source |
| Fasting blood sugar | 70–99 mg/dL normal | Pending source |
| Blood oxygen | 95–100 % normal | Pending source |
| Sleep | 7–9 h normal | Pending source |
| Mood | 4–5 normal on a 1–5 scale | App scale |

These are general adult clinical references used as a starting point. When a range is confirmed, set `"verified": true` and fill in `"source"` with the URL.

## Future body (`data/forecast.json`)

| System | Value | Source | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bones (spine and hips) | 1% to 1.5% density loss per month on 4–6 month missions | [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-of-spaceflight-induced-bone-changes/) | Confirmed |
| Bones with countermeasures | Less loss; exact effect not published | [OHS Online, 2012](https://ohsonline.com/articles/2012/08/27/nasa-more-hopeful-on-astronaut-fitness.aspx) | Pending number. Fill `effectFactor` (share of loss that remains, for example 0.4) |
| Muscles | Minimal losses with full exercise on the ISS | [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/esdmd/hhp/risk-of-impaired-performance-due-to-reduced-muscle-size-strength-and-endurance/) | Pending monthly rate |
| Heart and circulation | Qualitative | — | Pending source |
| Eyes and brain (SANS) | Qualitative | — | Pending source |
| Mind and sleep | Qualitative | [NASA five hazards](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards) | Pending measured effects |

Rules the app follows:

- A system only gets a number when the number has a source. Otherwise it is shown in words with a "No number yet" badge.
- Projections past the mission length a rate was measured on are marked as extrapolated.
- The green, yellow and red bands of Future body are chosen by the app to make change visible. They are not clinical thresholds.

## Radiation (`data/radiation.json`, `data/space_weather.json`)

| Item | Value | Source | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Career dose limit | Less than 600 mSv | [NASA Radiation Protection technical brief](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/radiation-protection-technical-brief-ochmo.pdf) | Confirmed |
| Storm shelter action | Shelter in crew quarters with extra water shielding when alerted | Same brief | Confirmed |
| Solar particle events | Live: last 30 days from the DONKI SEP API. Offline: 10 archived GOES events from 2024 | [NASA DONKI](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI/) | Confirmed |
| Daily dose rule | Yellow when a day's dose is more than 3 times the personal 7-day average | App rule | Pending review |
| Career bar colours | Yellow from 50%, red from 80% of the limit | App rule | Display only |

## The five hazards

| Hazard | What the app uses |
| --- | --- |
| Space radiation | Daily dosimeter reading, career dose against the 600 mSv limit, solar particle events from DONKI |
| Isolation and confinement | Sleep and mood |
| Distance from Earth | Message delay for the current location, shown with every red action; the app works offline |
| Gravity fields | Heart rate, blood pressure and the Future body bone projection |
| Hostile closed environment | Temperature, blood sugar and blood oxygen |
